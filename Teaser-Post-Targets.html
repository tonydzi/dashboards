I record a huge number of voice notes, and the old problem was simple: a note turns into text and then gets forgotten. What I want instead is for every voice note to turn into a task.

Right now the note gets transcribed and becomes a task in the chat. Next I want each important one to spawn its own session, and the system to decide on its own what it is: a post, a task for Claude, an idea to sit with. Still just a direction, but I like it.
